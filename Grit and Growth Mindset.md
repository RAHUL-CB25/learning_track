# Life Skills – Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1: Grit

Grit means having the determination to continue working toward a goal even when things become difficult. The video explains that success depends not only on talent or intelligence but also on hard work, patience, and perseverance.

## Question 2: Growth Mindset

A growth mindset is the belief that our abilities can improve through practice, effort, and learning. People with this mindset see challenges and mistakes as opportunities to improve.

## Question 3: Internal Locus of Control

### Internal Locus of Control

Internal locus of control means believing that our own decisions, actions, and efforts have an important role in determining the results we achieve in life.

### Key Points

- Appreciating effort helps people stay motivated.
- Recognizing hard work encourages people to keep trying.
- Focusing only on intelligence can reduce motivation.
- People praised only for being smart may avoid difficult tasks.
- Continuous effort and determination are important for long-term success.

## Question 4: Steps to Develop a Growth Mindset

- Set a clear and achievable goal.
- Identify the skills and knowledge required.
- Select suitable learning methods.
- Allocate regular time for learning.
- Practice and improve consistently.

## Question 5: My Ideas to Develop a Growth Mindset

- Set realistic goals every day.
- Focus on understanding concepts instead of rushing.
- Appreciate small improvements.
- Treat mistakes as learning opportunities.
- Accept difficult tasks and challenges.
- Take feedback positively and work on weaknesses.
- Share knowledge with others.
- Practice regularly.
- Stay patient when progress is slow.
- Remember that difficulties help us build stronger skills and confidence.
