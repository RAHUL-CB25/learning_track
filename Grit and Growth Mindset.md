# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1

* Grit means staying focused on a long-term goal and continuing even when things get difficult.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2

* Growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills through effort, practice, and learning from mistakes.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3

* It means believing that my actions and decisions can affect my results.
* I should focus on what I can control instead of blaming others or situations.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4

* Accept challenges.
* Keep practicing.
* Learn from mistakes.
* Take feedback positively.
* Focus on progress.
* Replace “I can't” with “I can't yet.”
* Keep trying when things get difficult.

## 5. My Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 5

* Practice coding regularly.
* Learn from my mistakes.
* Ask for feedback.
* Learn from people who are better than me.
* Break big goals into small steps.
* Track my progress.
* Stay consistent even after failure.
