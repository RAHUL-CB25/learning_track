# Learning Process

## What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a simple way to learn something by explaining it in easy words, as if you are teaching it to someone who knows nothing about the topic. If you can explain it clearly, it means you understand it well.

## What was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The idea that I liked the most is **Practice vs. Understanding**.

- Reading the same topic again and again may make it feel familiar, but it doesn't always mean you understand it.
- Trying to remember the answer without looking at your notes (active recall) helps you learn better and remember it for a longer time.

## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Focused Mode

This is when I give full attention to one problem and solve it step by step without getting distracted.

### Diffuse Mode

This happens when I take a short break or relax. During this time, my brain connects ideas in a different way, and sometimes I get a better solution.

## What are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

When I start learning something new, I follow these steps:

1. Break the topic into small and simple parts.
2. Learn the basics first so I can start practicing.
3. Remove things that waste my time or distract me.
4. Practice regularly until I become confident with the topic.

## What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

- Study in a quiet place without distractions.
- Spend less time on social media while studying.
- Focus on one task at a time.
- Explain what I learned in my own words.
- Practice by writing code and building small projects.
- Make a daily or weekly study plan and follow it.
- Keep learning in a simple and enjoyable way.
- Improve one skill at a time instead of trying to learn everything together.
- Share my progress with others and ask questions whenever I get stuck.
