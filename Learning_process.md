# Learning Process

## Question 1
### What is the Feynman Technique?

* Explain a topic in simple words as if you are teaching it to someone else.

## Question 2
### In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

* Taking breaks can help the brain connect ideas and find solutions.

## Question 3
### What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

* Active mode: Focusing on one problem.
* Diffused mode: Relaxing the mind and connecting different ideas.

## Question 4
### According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

* Break the topic into small parts.
* Learn the basics first.
* Remove distractions.
* Practice what you learn.
* Practice for at least 20 hours.
* Explain the topic in your own words.

## Question 5
### What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

* Focus completely while studying.
* Keep my phone on silent during work.
* Block distracting apps during study time.
* Track my study time regularly.
* Understand concepts instead of memorizing them.
* Explain concepts in my own words.
* Practice every concept by writing code.
* Complete assignments before deadlines.
